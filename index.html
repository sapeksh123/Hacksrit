About HACK SRIT

HACKSRIT 2025, presented by ID8DevHub in collaboration with Shri Ram College of Engineering, Jabalpur, is the inaugural edition of a thrilling hackathon focused on Artificial Intelligence (AI). Designed to empower students and tech enthusiasts to build real-world AI solutions, HACKSRIT offers a platform for innovation, collaboration, and impact.

Rules

  • All participants must register individually or as a team (maximum team size: 4 members).
  • Each team must submit an original idea during the idea submission phase.
  • Plagiarism of ideas or projects will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Teams must adhere to submission deadlines and final presentation guidelines.

Eligibility

  • Teams must bring their own laptops and development tools.
  • Open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students from any recognized college/university across India.
  • Participants must have a basic understanding of programming and AI/ML concepts.
  • Students from all disciplines are encouraged to participate — diversity in skills is a plus!

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in HackSRIT?

Hack SRIT is open to all college students across India. You must be at least 18 years old and have a valid college ID to participate.

How many members can be in a team?

Each team can have a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 members. Solo participation is not allowed.

Is there a registration fee?

No, participation in Hack SRIT is completely free of charge.

Will accommodation be provided?

Accommodation will not be provided. However, meals will be arranged for all participants during the hackathon.

Can we work on a project we've already started?

No, all projects must be developed from scratch during the hackathon. You can come with ideas, but the coding and implementation should start only after the hackathon begins.

What should I bring to the hackathon?

You should bring your laptop, charger, any hardware you plan to use for your project, and your college ID. Don't forget to bring personal items like toiletries and a change of clothes.

Will food be provided during the event?

Yes, meals and snacks will be provided throughout the hackathon at no additional cost.

How will the projects be judged?

Projects will be judged based on innovation, technical complexity, practicality, presentation, and adherence to the chosen track. A panel of industry experts and faculty members will evaluate all submissions.
